Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025  ·  Effective immediately

🖤 Overview

OpsFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered operations dashboard built by MVP Dashboard. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application at mvpopsflow.netlify.app.

By using OpsFlow, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

📋 Information we collect

We collect only what's necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information (name, email) via Google OAuth sign-in
  • Task and note data you create inside OpsFlow, stored in our database
  • Gmail data — subject lines and sender info of emails, accessed read-only to display in your dashboard
  • Google Calendar data — event titles and times, accessed read-only to display in your dashboard
  • Usage data — pages visited and actions taken, for improving the product

📧 Google API data (Gmail & Calendar)

OpsFlow uses the Google API to display your Gmail messages and Google Calendar events inside your dashboard. Regarding this data:

  • We access Gmail and Calendar data only to display it to you within OpsFlow
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your Gmail or Calendar data to any third party
  • We do not use Gmail or Calendar data for advertising or training AI models
  • We store only the minimum data needed to display your dashboard (e.g. email subjects, sender names, event titles)
  • You can revoke OpsFlow's access to your Google data at any time via your Google Account settings
OpsFlow's use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

🔒 How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the OpsFlow dashboard
  • Display your Gmail and Calendar data within your personal workspace
  • Save your tasks, notes, and settings between sessions
  • Improve the product based on usage patterns

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

🛡️ Data storage & security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a managed database platform with industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. We use HTTPS for all data transmission.

Google OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only to make authorized requests to the Google API on your behalf.

🍪 Cookies & local storage

OpsFlow uses cookies and browser local storage solely for authentication session management (keeping you logged in). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

👤 Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Revoke Google API permissions at any time via your Google Account
  • Export your tasks and notes data upon request

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

🔄 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of OpsFlow after changes means you accept the updated policy.
